Fine Print

  • LeShuttle pricing is per vehicle, each way. Booked Short Stay Saver: €144 outbound + €145 return = €289 total. Short Stay Saver applies because the trip is under 5 days.
  • Uneven split: Niels + Rune + Kilian + Jany drive out together (outbound split 4 ways = €41.64 pp). Niels + Rune + Wout drive back (return split 3 ways = €64.13 pp). Wout joins in Sheffield - he only pays for the return.
  • UK ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) is £16 per person, only required if you don't already hold a valid one. ETAs are valid for 2 years or until passport expiry, so if you've been to the UK since April 2025 you may already have one. Apply only via gov.uk or the official UK ETA app - avoid third-party sites that charge extra.
  • Round trip math: 1 200 km and 216 kWh already account for both directions. LeShuttle figures above are the full return.
  • Home charge before departure is counted as free (already on home tariff). Add ~€18 total (~€4.50 pp) if you want to count it at €0.30/kWh.
  • Consumption of 18 kWh/100 km is realistic for highway driving in November with 4 adults plus luggage. Summer or lighter load lands closer to 16.
  • Not included: hotel, food, drinks, comp entry fee, Sheffield parking, post-comp pints.
  • Timezones: UK is 1 h behind CET in November (no DST in either).
